## About the project

**Sinfonía Biótica** is an immersive VR experience that translates bioelectrical activity and the environment of trees, fungi, and other organisms into a 3D audiovisual space. The project brings together applied research, sound design, and technical development:

- **Biodata** — capture and monitoring with biodata devices  
- **Environmental & electrical signals** — integration of environmental and electrical variables  
- **Open data stack** — real-time storage and querying through custom infrastructure (database, API, and [open data portal](https://sinfoniabiotica.xyz/open-database/))  
- **Digital ecosystems** — scanning and post-production of ecosystems for use inside the experience  

Alongside the main experience, the team builds companion pieces and devices — including the so-called **“forest sentinels”** (*centinelas del bosque*) — that make biological signals tangible and tie them to a specific ecological context.

Learn more on the official site: **[sinfoniabiotica.xyz](https://sinfoniabiotica.xyz/)**
