# BIOTIC SYMPHONY XR WORKSHOP
## Artistic Creation with Living Organisms

### CONCEPT NOTE

---

## Overview

This immersive workshop explores the convergence of art, nature, and technology through the lens of bioart and extended reality. Participants will learn to transform living organisms data (Plants, fungi)  into active collaborators in the creative process, bridging the gap between the biological and digital realms.

It is an innovative hands-on workshop that reimagines the relationship between artists and the natural world. By employing biosensors and digital tools, participants will learn to perceive and amplify the invisible signals of plant life, translating biological processes into compelling audiovisual experiences.

he workshop combines three core elements:

1. **Bioacoustic Sensing**: Using specialized sensors to capture and amplify the micro-fluctuations and bioelectric signals of plants, making the inaudible audible.

2. **Digital Scanning & Photogrammetry**: Learning techniques to digitize real-world objects and spaces, creating virtual replicas that can be manipulated and integrated into digital environments.

3. **Audiovisual Synthesis**: Transforming biological data and digitized environments into immersive multimedia installations and performances.

## Workshop Description

**Biotic Symphony XR** is an innovative hands-on workshop that reimagines the relationship between artists and the natural world. By employing biosensors and digital tools, participants will learn to perceive and amplify the invisible signals of plant life, translating biological processes into compelling audiovisual experiences.

The workshop combines three core elements:

1. **Bioacoustic Sensing**: Using specialized sensors to capture and amplify the micro-fluctuations and bioelectric signals of plants, making the inaudible audible.

2. **Digital Scanning & Photogrammetry**: Learning techniques to digitize real-world objects and spaces, creating virtual replicas that can be manipulated and integrated into digital environments.

3. **Audiovisual Synthesis**: Transforming biological data and digitized environments into immersive multimedia installations and performances.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this workshop, participants will:

- Understand the fundamentals of biosensing and plant electrophysiology
- Gain hands-on experience with sensors and data acquisition systems
- Learn basic photogrammetry and 3D scanning techniques
- Develop skills in translating biological signals into sound and visuals
- Create their own bioart audiovisual piece
- Explore ethical considerations in working with living organisms as creative partners

## Target Audience

This workshop is designed for:
- Artists interested in bioart and new media
- Musicians exploring alternative controllers and generative processes
- Designers working with interactive installations
- Educators seeking innovative STEAM approaches
- Technologists curious about bio-digital interfaces
- Anyone passionate about the intersection of nature, art, and technology

No prior technical experience required. Beginners welcome!

## Workshop Structure

This is an **introductory 4-hour workshop** structured in two main parts:

### Part 1: Introduction to Bioart & Biosensing (90 minutes)
- Introduction to bioart: history, ethics, and contemporary practices
- Understanding plant bioelectricity and bioacoustic signals
- Presentation of the Biotic Symphony XR project
- Introduction to sensors and biosensing technology
- **Hands-on:** Setting up sensors with living plants

### Part 2: Digital Capture & Audiovisual Creation (90 minutes)
- Introduction to photogrammetry and 3D scanning techniques
- Demonstration of object and space digitization
- **Hands-on:** Creating a simple digital scan using smartphones
- Signal processing basics: from bio-data to audiovisual parameters
- **Demonstration:** Real-time audiovisual composition with plant signals

### Part 3: Integration & Creative Exploration (60 minutes)
- Integrating digital assets with bio-signals
- Creating a simple audiovisual piece
- **Group activity:** Collaborative bioart creation
- Q&A and discussion: possibilities and future applications
- Resources for continued learning

## Philosophical Framework

This workshop challenges anthropocentric perspectives by positioning plants and other organisms as collaborators rather than passive materials. We explore questions such as:

- Can we develop new modes of interspecies communication through technology?
- How do living systems express themselves when given technological "voices"?
- What does it mean to co-create with non-human entities?
- How can technology help us develop deeper ecological awareness?

## About the Methodology

Our approach emphasizes:
- **Experiential learning**: Learning by doing and experimenting
- **Ethical practice**: Respectful engagement with living organisms
- **Interdisciplinary thinking**: Bridging art, science, and technology
- **Creative exploration**: Encouraging experimentation and risk-taking
- **Community building**: Fostering connections among participants
